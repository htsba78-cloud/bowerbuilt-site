Tatum Support

Something not working, or a question about your account? Write to alex@itsbowerbuilt.com and a real person will answer. Tatum is made by one person, so replies usually come within a couple of days.

Reporting a problem from inside the app

The fastest way to report a bug is in the app itself: open the menu, choose Account, and use Report a Problem. That sends along which screen you were on and what device you're using, which usually makes the problem much easier to find.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the menu, go to Account, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete Account. You'll type your email address to confirm. It's immediate and permanent — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what gets removed.

Tatum identified my bottle incorrectly.

It happens — scanning depends on what's readable on the label. You can correct the bottle by hand in your bar, and reporting it through the app helps Tatum get better at that bottle for everyone.

I've hit a daily limit.

Each recommendation costs money to generate, so there's a cap on how many requests an account can make per day. It resets on a rolling basis — try again later.

I forgot my password.

Use the password reset on the sign-in screen. If the reset email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then write to us.

Can I use Tatum on my phone?

Yes — visit tatumthebartender.com in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. An iPhone app is in the works.
