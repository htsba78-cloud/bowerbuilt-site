Tatum Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Tatum is made by Bower Built LLC, a single-member company in Delaware, USA. This policy explains what Tatum collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. It covers the Tatum app and tatumthebartender.com.

The short version

  • Tatum keeps your account, your bar, your taste, and your conversations with Tatum — so it can recommend drinks you'll actually like.
  • Your data is never sold, rented, or used for advertising. There are no third-party trackers or analytics.
  • Bottle photos are sent to Anthropic to identify what's in them. They are only stored when Tatum doesn't recognize the bottle, and deleted once reviewed.
  • You can delete your account, and everything tied to it, from inside the app.

What Tatum collects

WhatWhy
Email address and passwordTo create and secure your account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored hashed — we never see them.
Confirmation that you are of legal drinking ageTatum is for adults 21 and over. We record that you confirmed this, and when.

Tatum does not collect your location, your contacts, or your device identifiers, and does not use cookies or similar technology for advertising or tracking.

Bottle photos

When you scan a bottle, the photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API so the bottle can be identified.

  • If Tatum recognizes the bottle, the photo is not stored. It is used to answer that one request and then discarded.
  • If Tatum does not recognize the bottle, the photo is stored so a person can confirm what it is before the bottle is added to Tatum's reference list. It is deleted once that review is done.
  • If you delete your account, your photos are deleted immediately, whether or not they have been reviewed.

Who your data is shared with

Tatum relies on three service providers. They process data so Tatum can function, and nothing more.

ProviderRole
AnthropicPowers Tatum's responses. Receives your messages to Tatum, the contents of your bar, your taste information, and bottle photos when you scan. Handled under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy.
SupabaseDatabase, account authentication, and photo storage.
RailwayHosting for the Tatum service.

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else. It may be disclosed if required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of users.

Artificial intelligence

Tatum's recommendations are generated by Anthropic's Claude models. To produce a recommendation, the information described above is sent to Anthropic's API and processed there. Tatum also keeps your ratings and corrections in its own database, which is how it adapts to your taste over time.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time: open the menu, go to Account, and choose Delete Account. You will be asked to type your email address to confirm. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your account removes:

  • Your login and email address
  • Your bar and your custom ingredients
  • Your taste profile, drink history, ratings and corrections
  • Your conversations with Tatum
  • Any bug reports you submitted
  • Your usage records
  • Any bottle photos you took

One thing is kept, with no link to you. When you scan a bottle, Tatum records what that bottle is — its name and category — which is how Tatum learns to recognize bottles for everyone. These records are factual information about products, not about you. When you delete your account, your identity is stripped from them permanently, and what remains cannot be traced back to you.

How long data is kept

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Unreviewed bottle photos are kept until they are reviewed. Usage records are kept on a rolling basis for fair-use limits and are routinely discarded. If you delete your account, your data is deleted at that time.

Security

Tatum is served over encrypted connections. Access to the database is restricted, and per-user rules keep one account from reading another's data. No system is perfectly secure, but your information is treated with care appropriate to what it is.

Children

Tatum is about alcoholic drinks and is intended only for adults of legal drinking age — 21 and over in the United States. It is not directed at children, and accounts are not knowingly created for anyone under the legal drinking age. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

Your choices

You can view and change your bar, taste information, and account details inside the app at any time, and you can delete your account as described above. If you would like a copy of your data, or have a question about how it is handled, email us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Significant changes will be communicated in the app.
